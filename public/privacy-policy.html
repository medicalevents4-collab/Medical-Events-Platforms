Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you visit or use the Medical Events Platform.

Effective date: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Medical Events Platform ("we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with https://medicalevents.online/ and the platform's features. Please do not submit information you are not authorised to share.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the platform, information may include:

  • Account details such as your name, email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Profile details and information you choose to add to the platform.
  • Content you create or submit, such as event information, posts, calendar entries, resources and messages.
  • Technical and usage information, such as browser/device details, log records, and security or diagnostic events.
  • When you choose Google sign-in, basic account information made available through Google's sign-in process, subject to the permissions shown to you.

2. How we use information

We may use information to create and manage accounts, provide platform features, display the content you submit, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, communicate about your account, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Legal basis and South African privacy rights

Where applicable, we process personal information in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and other applicable privacy laws. Depending on the circumstances, processing may be necessary to provide the service, comply with law, protect legitimate interests, or be based on your consent. You may request access to or correction/deletion of personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where consent is the basis, subject to legal requirements and exceptions.

4. Service providers and sharing

We may use service providers that help operate the platform, including hosting, authentication, database, security and communications providers. They may process information on our behalf to provide their services. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a legitimate business transfer. We do not control the separate privacy practices of third-party services you choose to use.

5. Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google processes information under Google's own privacy policy. We receive information needed to identify and authenticate your account, as described on Google's consent and sign-in screens. We use it to provide sign-in and account functionality; we do not use Google data for unrelated purposes.

6. Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain security and records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations, then delete or de-identify it where appropriate.

7. International processing

Some service providers may process or store information in countries outside South Africa. Where applicable, we take steps intended to ensure that any such processing is permitted by relevant data-protection law.

8. Children

The platform is not intended for children to use without appropriate authorisation and supervision. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can review the matter.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The current version will be published on this page with its effective date.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at kumanionline@gmail.com.

Note: This policy describes general platform practices and should be reviewed against the organisation's actual data handling and legal obligations before relying on it as final legal advice.
